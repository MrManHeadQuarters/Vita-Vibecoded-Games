About

Stairway to Heaven is a 3D round-based zombies shooter for the PS Vita, inspired by the Black Ops 2 Zombies mode. The whole map is an open-air white stairway floating in the sky: short flights of stairs with a landing on each floor.

Spend points to open debris and climb higher. Each landing holds two perks, one on each side. It's a fan-made homebrew tribute and isn't affiliated with the makers of Call of Duty.

Features

  • Round-based zombie waves on a floor-by-floor climb
  • Buyable debris with costs that escalate steeply on each floor
  • Two uniquely named perks per landing, working like their BO2 counterparts
  • Mystery-box-style roulette that stays on the spawn platform for good
  • Pack-a-Punch-style weapon upgrader
  • Zombies break barricades on the spawn platform and rise from the ground elsewhere
  • Offline only: a single PLAY button, no online mode
