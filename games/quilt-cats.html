Quilt Cats

Meowdoku-like cat logic puzzle

About

Quilt Cats is a Meowdoku-like game for the PS Vita: a cat logic puzzle in the same style as the phone game Meowdoku, rebuilt for the Vita's buttons. Every level is a quilt split into coloured patches, and your job is to place cats so that every row, every column and every patch gets exactly one cat.

Mark the squares you've ruled out with an X, and keep an eye on your three hearts: mistakes cost a life. Finish a quilt to get a star rating, your time and your best time. It's a fan-made homebrew tribute and isn't affiliated with the makers of Meowdoku.

Features

  • One cat per row, per column and per colour patch
  • Numbered levels with difficulty labels and a level list
  • Three hearts per level: mistakes cost a life
  • Hint button, plus undo and redo
  • Mark ruled-out squares with an X, or erase them
  • Win screen with a star rating, time, best time, hints used and lives left
