AC-130 Spectre

3D thermal-view gunship shooter
⬇ Download VPK 4.4 MB

About

AC-130 Spectre puts you in the gunner's seat. The camera orbits the map as the plane circles overhead, with a real lens zoom so you can pull in close on the action.

Little robot targets show up on the thermal feed. They hide inside buildings when shot at and run out when their building is hit or destroyed, while SAM sites stay on the map as threats you have to take out to survive. Its look is inspired by a Roblox game; it isn't affiliated with it.

Features

  • 3D top-down orbiting camera with lens-style zoom
  • Thermal view, with robot targets that hide in and flee from buildings
  • Buildings that break apart where they're hit, not all at once
  • Robots that come apart according to where they're hit, with wires showing on torn-off limbs
  • SAM sites to destroy to stay alive
  • Upgrade skill tree for the AC-130
  • End-of-mission memorial montage of the robots you took down
