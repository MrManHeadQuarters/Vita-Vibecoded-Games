About

Vita Bowl is a Retro Bowl-style football game made for the PS Vita. It keeps things simple on purpose: a 12-player card roster, four-bar attributes, short quarters, and passing plays that run mostly on their own so you can focus on the big moments.

It's a fan-made homebrew tribute, not affiliated with or endorsed by the makers of Retro Bowl.

Features

  • All 32 teams in their colours, with simulated seasons
  • Draft and scouting between seasons, plus XP progression
  • 12-player card roster with simple four-bar attributes
  • Short 3–5 minute quarters, with adjustable drive direction
